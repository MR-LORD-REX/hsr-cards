Synergy
  • Mortenax Blade - Mortenax Blade is currently Acheron's strongest Nihility teammate by far, outclassing all other Nihility options and elevating her team's performance to new heights thanks to his powerful DMG amplification and debuff application frequency. His Zone allows any ally attack to apply debuffs, enabling Follow-up attackers to rapidly generate stacks for Acheron, and he can also apply debuffs during enemy actions if they attack him, making him the best stack generator. He also increases the team's Ultimate DMG dealt and enemies' DMG Taken, and reduces enemies' DEF.
  • Ashveil - Incredibly strong Sub-DPS, especially when paired with Mortenax Blade, and can reduce all enemies' DEF by 40%. Ashveil's frequent Follow-up ATKs generate Slashed Dream points for Acheron (and Charges for Mortenax Blade), deal decent DMG himself, and increase Acheron's CRIT DMG by 40% on all attacks, with an extra 80% on Follow-up ATKs. Despite not following the Path of Nihility, Ashveil is regarded as one of her best teammates and is often worth running over another Nihility character.
  • Robin Summeretto - Robin • Summeretto provides a massive ATK buff for Acheron on top of 40% DEF Ignore and a 20% DMG Taken increase on enemies. Although Acheron doesn't benefit from Energy, Robin • Summeretto is still one of her best teammates, as her Ultimate's on-demand Action Advance can reset Acheron's powerful Follow-up ATK.
  • Welt - Welt is a strong teammate to fulfil Acheron's A4 Nihility teammate requirements thanks to his immense DMG amplification debuffs: 40% DEF Reduction and 100% DMG boost after some ramp up, with an additional 30% All-Type RES Reduction if Welt is E4. More with a debuffing Light Cone. Although he is not best at generating stacks for Acheron, he can help with alleviating sustain pressure by delaying and slowing enemies and sometimes also use his Ultimate to cancel enemies' Extra Actions. However, his main ability to delay enemies' action can cause some anti-synergy with Jiaoqiu/Mortenax Blade, owing to the fact that they require enemies taking action while Welt is actively preventing it.
  • Hyacine - Strong healing sustain with high attack frequency, thanks to her naturally high SPD and Little Ica following up her actions with attacks. Best with Robin • Summeretto, as Hyacine's HP buff also results in a higher ATK buff for Acheron.
  • Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae - Shielding sustain who provides Acheron with an ATK buff and can launch Follow-up ATKs for stacks generation, though he may struggle alongside Mortenax Blade, who needs healing in long fights. This can be solved with his Signature Light Cone.
  • Cipher - Permanently increases DMG Taken by all enemies by 40% while she's on field, and can apply more debuffs than usual thanks to her Follow-up ATK. Cipher can also record a portion of DMG dealt by Acheron to unleash all of it when you need for massive True DMG.
  • Jiaoqiu - Jiaoqiu is an Ultimate DMG amplifier capable of applying debuffs during enemy actions. He can apply a 50% Vulnerability debuff for Ultimate DMG while still amplifying other DMG types by 35%.
  • Tribbie - Premier AoE support who launches a Follow-up ATK when allies use Ultimates, making her a decent stack generator for Acheron when Mortenax Blade is on the team.
