Eidolon Information

  • E1 - Significantly boosts her personal DMG, and offers additional Ultimate Charges, though this does not address her primary weakness. Her personal DMG outside of Castorice/Evernight team is minimal; don't expect this to change much, if at all. For the additional Ultimate Charges, her main issue is still getting her first Ultimate, whereas this Eidolon Ultimate refund is only after she has used her Ultimate multiple times. Not recommended to stop at Eidolon 1, only get this if going for Eidolon 2.
  • E2 - Grants her much faster access to her Ultimate, and doubles her True DMG buff up to 48%. For some teams, this Eidolon is very powerful, helping Cyrene to use her Ultimate within a much more reasonable timeframe as her Ultimate Charge generation is slow outside of Mono-Remembrance teams. Recommended early investment stopping point.
  • E4 - Enables her Main DPS potential after a long period of ramp up. Can increase her each Bounce instance from 60% of her HP to 204% after ~24 Ultimates.
  • E6 - Transform her first Ultimate into a mini-Robin Ultimate with a teamwide 100% Action Advance, and permanently decreases all enemies' DEF by 20% after the initial 2 Ultimates. Then, every three Ultimates will trigger a teamwide 24% Action Advance.
Signature vs Eidolons

Are you wondering whether you should pull for Cyrene Eidolons or her Light Cone? Then this section is for you!

Cyrene E2 > Cyrene S1 > Cyrene E1

  1. Cyrene E2 - Gives her 12 additional Recollection stacks at the start of battle, helping Cyrene to use her Ultimate within a much more reasonable timeframe. For teams that do not have other Remembrance allies, it is recommended to aim for Eidolon 2 before her S1.
  2. Cyrene S1 - S1 offers strong DMG amplification especially in CRIT-scaling teams, and helps her with reaching SPD thresholds, though it lacks utilities like giving her faster access to her Ultimate. Teams that can recharge her Ultimate reliably through memosprites can consider the Light Cone before Eidolons.
  3. Cyrene E1 - Gives 6 Recollection points after the first 2 Ultimates and then every third Ultimate, which can be especially useful with One-time effect triggers like Mydei and Anaxa. However, her main weakness with slow Ultimate charging rate outside of Mono-Remembrance teams still remain, as this refund is only after she gets her initial Ultimate. Only go for E1 if aiming for E2, not recommended to stop at E1.
Synergy
  • Hyacine - The fastest unit to charge Cyrene's Ultimate, thanks to her naturally reaching high SPD and her actions always being followed by Little Ica for 2 Recollection stacks. After Cyrene uses her Ultimate, Hyacine will automatically gain "Ode to Sky" without having to be actively targeted. The buff increases the rate of the healing tally for Little Ica's Memosprite Skill DMG (not the healing output).
  • Aglaea - Cyrene fixes Aglaea's SPD ramp up time by instantly stacking her Memosprite Talent to the max, but since her Ultimate takes a while to generate this is not useful unless Aglaea exits her Supreme Stance. However, Cyrene's other amplification effects are potent and help with Aglaea's high energy cost, as she can repeatedly use her Ultimate on Aglaea to restore 70 Energy.
  • Mydei - Every use of Cyrene's Ultimate can trigger Mydei's Godslayer Be Gods attack while also massively boosting its' CRIT DMG. He is no longer as dependent on HP loss to dish out his highest damaging ability, and can use it exactly when you need by timing Cyrene's Ultimate.
  • Tribbie - Cyrene provides minor buffs for Tribbie's DMG but it is only for herself. For that reason, it is generally not worth casting the buff on Tribbie unless there are no other good targets. Tribbie is still a strong support in general even without the buffs, and being a Chrysos Heir can increase Cyrene's Ultimate stacks at the start of battle.
  • Castorice - Cyrene is the last Remembrance teammate to complete Castorice teams. Despite her Memosprite being untargetable, it will always mirror Cyrene's HP fluctations, resulting in additional Newbud generation for Castorice. Her buffs allow Castorice to overcharge her Ultimate up to 200%, and increases her detonation attack damage based on overflowed Newbud, stronger against 2 or less targets. In multi-target situations, it is not recommended to overcharge her Ultimate, as the majority of Netherwing's DMG would be from the Breath attacks, whereas it is unaffected by overflowed Newbud. Against 2 or less targets, Cyrene will massively boost the Bounce DMG from 40% per instance, up to 112% at max overflow, resulting in impressive Single-Target damage. Keep in mind the condition is checked when using her Ultimate, and not when the attack occurs
  • Anaxa - Cyrene offers beneficial buffs for both Anaxa's Sub DPS and Main DPS playstyle, but it's recommended to stick to Main DPS Anaxa with Cyrene. Every Ultimate will grant Anaxa an immediate action, a Skill Point, and additional Bounce instances for his Skill which can be duplicated by Cerydra. Lastly, for both of his playstyles, Cyrene's buffs also provide a large increase in Skill DMG for other Erudition allies.
  • Cipher - Cyrene can amplify Cipher's debuffs throughout the whole fight, further reducing the primary/other target's DEF by 20%/12%. Repeatable effects like Mydei's and Anaxa's are generally preferred over this.
  • Phainon - Cyrene is overall a comfortable support replacement for Phainon, although she can suffer from slow Ultimate startup time without other Remembrance characters on the team. Cyrene allows for Phainon to never leave his Ultimate, continuosly refreshing his Extra Turns and recovering Scourge while doing so, and also adding minor Fire DMG instances to his attack alongside CRIT buffs.
  • Hysilens - Despite weird wording, Cyrene's buff on Hysilens is mostly permanent, enabling Hysilens to detonate DoT debuffs via her Basic ATK/Skill and also increasing her DMG dealt by a whooping 120%. With this, her Skill can hit all 5 targets on the field and trigger her own Ultimate Zone effect. However, reapplying Cyrene's buff on Hysilens will regenerate 60 Energy for her Ultimate to detonating DoT debuffs and recover Skill Points.
  • Cerydra - Cyrene reduces the amount hit required to trigger Coup de Main from 6 to 5, and additionally increases the target's CRIT DMG by 30%.
  • March 7th • Evernight - Cyrene has a permanent buff on Evernight that improves her CRIT DMG scaling by an additional 12%, increases her Memoria generation and Memosprite Skill DMG. The mono-Remembrance team members all deal considerable damage, and boosting Evernight's own amplification effect is a great buff to further reinforce this.
  • Dan Heng • Permansor Terrae - With Cyrene on the team, Permansor Terrae does not need to be actively targeted by Cyrene's Memosprite Skill, and will additionally increase the Bondmate's DMG dealt by 24% whilst also providing even more Bondmate based DMG multipliers. Can also use Cyrene's Memosprite Skill on Permansor Terrae to trigger an emergency Follow-up ATK and Shield.
